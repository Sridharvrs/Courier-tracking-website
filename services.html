International air

128 destination countries with customs milestones baked into the same timeline — export scan, clearance, duty, import handover.

  • 2–5 business days typical
  • DDP & DAP incoterms

Cold chain

−25°C to +8°C corridors for pharma, vaccines and gourmet food. Sensor breach alerts fire before the threshold, not after.
