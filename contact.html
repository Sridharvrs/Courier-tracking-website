Answers that solve 70% of tickets

My parcel shows delivered but I did not receive it

Open the shipment and check the proof photo and GPS stamp. If the location is wrong, raise a claim from the same screen — the hub manager gets it in six hours.

Can I change the delivery address mid-route?

Yes, until the "out for delivery" scan. After that the rider must attempt the original address once before a redirect is allowed.

How do I add a teammate to my workspace?

Settings → Team → Invite. Choose Admin, Ops or Read-only. Seats are free on every plan.
